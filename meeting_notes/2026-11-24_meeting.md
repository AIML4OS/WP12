**Notes and action points:**

The group continued working in the Miro board.

*What makes NSIs "unique" – characteristic patterns for official statistics*

The group collected the following points:
- High (too high) use of databases, often with "raw"/direct access
- Many statistical programmes lack separation/isolation of dev, test and prod environments
- What it takes to maintain trust
- Part of the ESS
- Unknown requirements on reproducibility
- Explanation is required (transparency)
- Staff need transparency in technology
- Specialists as internal users
- External expectations on explainability
- Different levels of criticality in different statistical activities
- Complex data flows
- Many rules to follow
- Predictable and traceable production process
- Frequency: a one-off exercise is usually not relevant
- We are a reference for media, citizens and organisations
- Provider for LLMs as well as user
- Quality of published data
- Access to administrative data
- Provider of data for research
- Coordinated metadata
- The only source of official data
- Lots of data and metadata experience
- Security of personal data/microdata etc. is paramount

These were roughly clustered into four groups:
- External requirements and conditions
- Requirements arising from who our internal users of systems are and their competences
- Technical conditions
- Data and metadata related conditions

*Target groups*

After prioritisation, the report will primarily target:
- Tech lead/CTO
- IT architect
- Data scientist/methodologist

*Specific topics (revised list)*

- Bring your own AI
- Security
- Hosting environments for LLMs
- Attack vectors
- AI gateways
- Where does the model run
- Data privacy perspective
- Testability/safe spaces
- Test processes/evaluation
- Risk, mitigating hallucinations etc.
- Cost considerations
- Open source/commercial solutions
- Sovereignty
- Human in the loop
- Autonomy of the system
- Level of autonomy vs safety
- Tool-calling techniques
- Agentic skills
- ARD (discoverability)
- LLM choice
- Shareable artefacts in the statistical community

*Structure*

- The group decided to wait with the structure of the report, so as not to lock it in too early. The focus is on the content first; the structure will then be shaped to fit the content and the specific topics that need to be covered.

*Formats of the deliverable*

- A report is required to comply with the project application.
- Other formats/structures mentioned:
  - A wiki, e.g. on GitHub (GitHub Pages or similar)
  - One or more AI-ready formats, such as agentic skills, a chatbot, or data better structured for RAG applications
- Whether some material should go into the Knowledge repository that another work package is working on
- Whether there is material suitable for training, i.e. in a more packaged format
- How the experiences from working in SSPCloud can be included – for example as a separate report, or as part of the format or content of other deliverables
